---

## 1. Scope & Character of the Document
This document is a statutory regulatory direction issued by the Reserve Bank of India under Sections 21 and 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. It sets binding Priority Sector Lending (PSL) targets, sub-targets, and classification criteria for scheduled commercial banks, regional rural banks (RRBs), small finance banks (SFBs), and urban cooperative banks (UCBs).

---

## 2. Specific Relevance to Rural Enterprise Advisor

### 2.1 Directly Relevant Sections (Active in our Advisory Framework)
1. **Chapter I, Para 4(i) (Page 5) — Allied Activities Definition:**
   * *"Allied activities i.e. activities allied to agriculture shall include dairy, fisheries, animal husbandry, poultry, bee-keeping, sericulture and similar activities."*
   * **Relevance:** Confirms our agricultural diversification classifications (Dairy and Poultry) qualify for direct agricultural lending norms under banking regulations.
2. **Chapter III, Para 10.2 (Page 15) — Khadi and Village Industries (KVI):**
   * *"(i) All loans to units in the Khadi and Village Industries sector, which shall be categorised as lending to micro enterprises."*
   * **Relevance:** Grounding for PMEGP schemes: any credit sanctioned to a KVI/PMEGP unit is mandatorily scored as Micro-Enterprise PSL by commercial banks, eliminating bank pushback regarding priority sector quotas.
3. **Chapter II, Para 7.1 — Mandatory Micro-Enterprise PSL Sub-Target:**
   * Domestic scheduled commercial banks and foreign banks must allocate a mandatory sub-target of **7.5% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC)** specifically to Micro Enterprises.
   * **Relevance:** Informs rural micro-entrepreneurs that public and private banks have statutory pressure to finance eligible micro-enterprises.

## 3. Sections NOT Relevant to our First Pilot (Out of Scope)
* **Para 11:** Export Credit.
* **Para 12:** Education Loans.
* **Para 13:** Housing Credit (individual housing limits).
* **Para 14 & 15:** Social Infrastructure and Renewable Energy large installations.
* **Annex V:** Formulae for bank penalty deposits under RIDF for priority sector shortfalls.

## 4. Verdict for Phase 1 Pilot Migration
* **Verdict:** **PARTIALLY APPLICABLE AS REGULATORY REFERENCE, NOT A DIRECT DATA PIPELINE SOURCE.**
* Unlike NABARD (which provides project cost itemizations) or PMEGP guidelines (which provide subsidy percentages), the RBI Master Direction does not establish consumer interest rates or individual project asset costs.
* **Recommendation:** Retain in `data/raw/rbi/` as a regulatory compliance benchmark cited in the DPR (demonstrating bankability under RBI PSL guidelines), but do not feed numerical variables into the financial engine.
